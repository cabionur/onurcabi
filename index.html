About

A cinematographer who finds humanity in the unseen

Born in Eskişehir, Turkey in 1991. Graduated from New York Film Academy, Los Angeles. Over the past decade, Onur Cabi has traveled the world with a camera — seeking out the stories that live at the edges.

His work spans commercials, music videos, documentary and feature films. His 2016 debut documentary White Potion — about the lives of people with albinism in Tanzania — went on to win Best Documentary at 13 international festivals.

In June 2022, he completed a documentary project on the water crisis in Kenya, following women and children on their daily water journeys across scorching landscapes.
